更新日期：2026年10月31日。开发者：Ming Kong。

本机记录与处理

录音、转写、笔记、附件、事项和可选地点默认保存在你的设备上。应用没有开发者账号、广告追踪或开发者自建数据收集服务，不自动向开发者上传录音或文字。语音识别、翻译及AI能力依赖Apple系统服务、设备与语言资源；系统资源下载、备份和你选用的服务遵循各自政策。

权限由你控制

麦克风用于主动录音；语音识别用于转写。通知、提醒事项和闹钟用于你选择的事项与提醒。位置记录默认关闭，开启后在开始录音时尝试获取一次并保存在本机，不持续追踪。照片仅取用你选择的内容，相机在主动拍照时请求权限。你可以在系统设置中管理权限。

购买和权益

订阅、购买、退款及优惠码由Apple处理。应用通过StoreKit核验交易以判断Plus权益，开发者不直接取得你的支付卡信息。恢复购买需要连接App Store。永久权益与订阅共存时，订阅续费仍应由你在Apple账户中管理。

分享、导出与删除

只有你选择分享或导出时，选定内容才交给所选服务或接收方。应用内删除不会删除已经导出的副本；系统备份、云盘和第三方服务中的副本由对应设置管理。应用中的记录可通过最近删除功能管理。

支持与联系

联系 mkong.musician@gmail.com。请勿主动发送私人录音或敏感笔记。你主动发送的支持邮件及附件仅用于处理请求；如需删除，请通过该邮箱联系。

Privacy policy — English

MomentNotes stores recordings, transcripts, notes, attachments, tasks and optional locations on your device by default. It has no developer account, advertising tracking, or developer-operated data collection service, and does not automatically upload recordings or text to the developer. Apple speech, translation, AI, resource downloads and system backups are subject to Apple services and your settings.

Microphone, speech, notification, reminder, alarm and camera permissions serve the actions you choose. Location recording is off by default; when enabled it attempts one location lookup when a recording starts, stores it locally and does not continuously track you. Photo access is limited to selected photos.

Apple handles payments, subscriptions, refunds and offer codes. StoreKit verifies entitlement transactions; the developer does not receive payment card details. Sharing and exporting occur at your request and the selected service handles those copies. Deleting a local note does not recall exported copies or backups.

For support, email mkong.musician@gmail.com without private recordings or sensitive notes. Information you voluntarily send in support messages is used to address the request; contact the same address for deletion requests.
