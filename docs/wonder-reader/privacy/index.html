Wonder Reader 隐私说明

更新日期：2026-09-23。开发者：Ming Kong。联系：mkong.musician@gmail.com。

本说明适用于 Wonder Reader 的 Mac App Store 版本。普通本地阅读与用户主动启用的在线功能具有不同的数据处理方式。

本地文档与模型

导入的文档、阅读位置、书签、备注和处理结果保存在本机应用存储中。系统语音、Apple OCR 和本地 AI 模型处理在设备上进行。应用没有开发者运营的文档上传或诊断上传后台，也未集成广告、跨应用跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。本机日志可能记录运行状态和错误。

可选模型从 Hugging Face 及其下载重定向/CDN 获取。下载服务可看到连接信息，例如 IP 地址；模型下载本身不上传书籍内容。模型占用的存储空间与删除操作可在应用设置中管理。

可选在线语音与 AI

启用 Microsoft 在线语音时，需要朗读的文字、语音选择、语速和音高参数会直接发送到 Microsoft 语音服务。服务商能够看到网络连接信息。应用会保留有限的本地音频缓存并定期淘汰。

连接自己的 OpenAI/Codex 账号并执行在线任务时，选定范围的文本或图片及处理指令会发送到相应 OpenAI 服务，结果返回后在本地保存。账号登录由服务商处理，凭据使用受支持的系统凭据存储。开发者不通过自建代理接收这些内容。第三方对账号、请求、日志和保留期限的处理由其政策及账号设置决定；请勿将本地处理功能的隐私范围套用于在线功能。

参阅 Microsoft 隐私声明及 OpenAI 隐私政策。

同步、备份、购买与更新

同步或备份仅使用你主动选择的文件夹，可包含文档原件、阅读位置、书签备注及处理结果，不包含模型。若选择 iCloud Drive 文件夹，由 Apple 系统同步，适用你的 iCloud 设置与 Apple 政策；也可以选择本地文件夹。

自愿支持购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用验证交易并在本地记录支持状态；开发者没有支付后台，不接收你的银行卡信息。所有阅读功能不依赖支持购买。

启动更新检查向 Apple 查询应用版本及商店地区，升级时打开 App Store。商店版不会使用网站版 Sparkle 更新源来安装程序。

你的选择

你可以只使用本地功能，不启用在线语音或在线 AI；可以断开账号、管理模型和删除自己的书籍与处理结果。应用内删除、系统备份以及第三方服务的保留行为可能不同；iCloud 文件和第三方账号数据请同时通过相应服务管理。隐私问题可通过上述邮箱联系开发者。

Wonder Reader Privacy Notice

Updated September 23, 2026. Developer: Ming Kong. Contact: mkong.musician@gmail.com.

This notice covers the Mac App Store edition. Local reading and optional online features handle data differently.

Local documents and models

Imported documents, reading positions, bookmarks, notes and results are stored locally. System speech, Apple OCR and local AI run on the device. The app has no developer-operated document or diagnostic upload backend and integrates no advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics SDK. Local operating-system logs may contain operational status and errors.

Optional models download from Hugging Face and its redirects/CDNs. Download services receive connection information such as IP addresses; model downloads do not upload books. Models can be managed in Settings.

Optional online services

Microsoft online speech sends narration text, voice selection, rate and pitch directly to Microsoft. Microsoft receives network connection information. The app maintains a limited, periodically evicted local audio cache.

When you connect your OpenAI/Codex account and request an online task, selected text or images and instructions are sent to the corresponding OpenAI service. Returned results are saved locally. Authentication is handled by the provider and credentials use supported system credential storage. There is no developer-operated content proxy. Provider account settings and policies govern service-side processing and retention, including logs; local-only privacy properties do not apply to these online features.

See the Microsoft Privacy Statement and OpenAI Privacy Policy.

Sync, backups, purchases and updates

Sync and backups use folders you explicitly select. They may contain original documents, reading positions, bookmarks, notes and results, but not models. Apple synchronizes selected iCloud Drive folders according to your iCloud settings and Apple's policies. Local folders are also supported.

Optional support purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit. The app verifies transactions and records support status locally. The developer operates no payment backend and does not receive payment-card details. Reading features do not require a support purchase.

Launch update checks query Apple using the app identifier and storefront country, and open the App Store for upgrades. The store edition does not install executables through the website edition's Sparkle feed.

Your choices

You can use local features without enabling online speech or AI, disconnect your account, manage models, and remove your books and results. App deletion, system backups and provider retention differ; also manage iCloud files and provider account data through those services. Contact the developer at the email above with privacy questions.
