Wonder Reader

A free Mac reader designed for accessible reading, with text-to-speech, OCR, and optional local AI translation and summaries.

0.1.8 · Build 47 · Free full version

Download and install

For Apple silicon Macs (M1 or later), running macOS 14 or later. Intel Macs are not supported.

Download Wonder Reader free (ZIP, about 51.4 MB)

  1. Download and unzip the file.
  2. Drag Wonder Reader.app into Applications.
  3. Open the app to start reading.

The app is Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and includes a stapled notarization ticket. No account or UDID registration is required.

No subscription or paid unlock is required. AI model downloads are optional and separate from the app download.

Installation instructions and keyboard shortcuts

Read first; add models when you need them

Ordinary reading, system text-to-speech, and Apple OCR work without downloading an AI model. Navigate the table of contents, add bookmarks, and return to your saved reading position.

The original text is read-only. Processing results are kept separately, so you can refer back to the source when checking a translation or summary.

Optional AI translation, summaries, and model-based recognition use downloaded models locally on your Mac. Choose Low, Mid, High, or Advanced in Settings → Models; download, enable, or delete models there. Low is about 867 MB; actual download sizes are shown in the app.

Internet access is needed to download models. You do not need Python, Homebrew, or a manually configured model runtime.

Build 47: repair for updater launch failures

Restores missing executable permissions in the packaged updater helpers. No new shortcuts; existing reading, title, export and model features remain.

Affected older versions need one manual replacement: quit Wonder Reader normally, extract this ZIP, drag Wonder Reader.app into Applications and replace the old app, then reopen it. Do not delete your library, models, settings or Application Support. No uninstall is needed.

The broken old helper cannot reliably install its own repair. All four helper permissions have been verified; a full subsequent cross-version upgrade still awaits isolated end-to-end validation.

Current limitations

Physical shortcuts and sustained VoiceOver use of the new title controls still require acceptance testing.

Real iCloud uploads, downloads after cloud eviction, large libraries under low disk space and long VoiceOver sessions remain incompletely tested. Full Word/Pages interoperability, complex MOBI multimedia and complex PDF layouts are not comprehensively validated.

AI model combinations, cancellation races, human sound feedback, clean-Mac installation and complete cross-version automatic upgrades still have outstanding acceptance work. Check OCR and AI results against the original; the previously reported close-book issue remains open.
